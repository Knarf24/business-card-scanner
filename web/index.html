📇 Business Card Scanner

After a meeting or an expo you come home with a pile of business cards, and typing each one into your phone takes ages, so most get lost in a drawer. Here you snap or upload a card, the app reads it and fills in name, job, company, phone numbers, email, website and address. Check it, then save it straight to your phone's contacts or download all your cards as a spreadsheet.

🐍 The card reading logic is Python running right here in your browser with Pyodide. 🔒 Your cards never leave your device.

How it works

    
    
      
  1. Clean up the photo (GeeksforGeeks Text Detection and Extraction using OpenCV and OCR):
        Python with NumPy turns the picture grey using the colour with the most contrast, flips dark cards so text
        is dark on light, evens out shadows and glare, and cuts the card out of the table around it.
    2. 
      
  2. Read the text (GeeksforGeeks Visiting Card Scanner GUI): the Tesseract text reader
        (Tesseract.js, running in your browser) reads each line and how big it is.
    3. 
      
  3. Find the details (GeeksforGeeks Email Id Extractor): Python finds emails, websites and phone
        numbers with patterns, sorts phones into mobile, office and fax from labels like "Cell" or "Fax" (or the
        number itself), picks the address lines, the job title and company from keywords, and the name from the
        biggest "First Last" line, checked against the email address.
    4. 
      
  4. Save it: Python writes a vCard (opens in the Contacts app on iPhone and Android, and in Outlook
        and Gmail) or a CSV spreadsheet for Excel and Google Sheets. It warns you if the person is already saved.
    5. 
    

    
Tips: take the photo straight on, in good light, with the card filling most of the picture.
      If the text is sideways, press Turn. English cards read best.

    
🔒 Privacy: everything happens on your device. Pictures and contacts are never uploaded;
      saved contacts stay in this browser until you delete them.
